how we’ll work together

what you get

Scope is fixed before anything is filmed. Every deliverable arrives as a vertical 9:16 master plus a captioned cut, ready to upload or hand to a media buyer.

  1. 01

    Scripting

    Hook, body, and call-to-action written to your angle and offer. Sent for approval before anything is filmed.

  2. 02

    Product demo

    Close-up, well-lit sequences that show the product actually working — texture, scale, application, result.

  3. 03

    Unboxing

    First-open reaction filmed clean, with packaging detail shots you can also pull as stills for static ads.

  4. 04

    Testimonial

    On-camera, first-person, conversational. Written from your real reviews so the claims stay inside what you can substantiate.

  5. 05

    Voiceover

    Recorded VO over b-roll or your existing footage. Useful when you need volume without new on-camera days.

  6. 06

    Hook variants

    Three to five alternate openings on the same body, cut as separate files so your media buyer can test the first two seconds.

  7. 07

    Raw footage delivery

    Unedited files handed over alongside the cut, so your in-house team can re-edit for other placements.

who you’re hiring

about

I’m Samantha, a UGC video creator in San Diego. I shoot vertical video for beauty, wellness, and home brands — the kind that reads as a real recommendation rather than an ad, because it is filmed the way I would film it for myself.

Everything is shot in my apartment and around the coast here: iPhone 15 Pro on a gimbal, natural window light with two Amaran 60x panels when the marine layer wins, a DJI Mic 2 on a lav, cut in Premiere Pro.

You get the first cut in 5–7 business days from the moment the product lands. I only take on categories I actually buy in, which is why the routines on camera are the ones I was already doing.
